What is assume balance?

A way to own a home by taking over an existing payment plan.

Pay the cash-out

This covers what the previous owner has already paid.

Take over the monthly payment

You continue the existing amortization for the remaining years.

Own the property

Once the paperwork is done and the balance is paid, it is yours.
